# CHNUG #5 - Thu, 12th of November 2026 @ Cisco


The Swiss Network User Group (CHNUG) is back for its fifth edition, and this time we’re heading to Wallisellen, where Cisco is kindly hosting us at their office!

Join us for an evening of technical talks covering network automation, network engineering, and how AI is starting to change the way we build and test networks. As always, there will be plenty of room for lively discussions and time to connect with fellow network engineers.

Cisco is also generously sponsoring food and drinks, so stick around after the talks for dinner and good conversation. On the menu: the Swiss classic Ghackets mit Hörnli, with vegetarian Bami Goreng as an alternative!

## Location
Cisco Systems (Switzerland) GmbH, Richtistrasse 7, 8304 Wallisellen ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/jSAnYoKF76Q76JVv6))
